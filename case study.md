Case Study
AI Teammate Platform for Supply Chain Operations
Overview
This case study is intended to evaluate how you approach ambiguous product problems, prioritize opportunities,
and deliver the product from 0 to 1.
There is no single correct answer. We are more interested in your thinking process than the final deliverables.
Reasonable assumptions are encouraged. If information is missing, state your assumptions explicitly rather than
trying to avoid uncertainty.
You are welcome to use AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Gemini) during your preparation. During the interview, we will
discuss your assumptions, reasoning, tradeoffs, and product decisions in depth.
Background
Our organization supports global supply chain operations involving manufacturing partners, procurement,
planning, logistics, operations management, and executive leadership.
Employees spend significant time every day:
• Searching for information across multiple systems
• Reading emails and reports
• Consolidating operational updates
• Investigating issues
• Coordinating with multiple stakeholders
• Tracking action items
• Preparing management reports
Although dashboards and reporting tools exist, users still rely heavily on manual work and human coordination to
make decisions and execute daily operations.
Recent advances in Generative AI provide an opportunity to rethink how enterprise software should work— not
simply by adding an AI chatbot, but by creating an intelligent digital teammate that actively helps employees
accomplish their work.
Your role is to define the first version of this next-generation platform.
Product Vision
The long-term vision is to build an AI platform that serves every employee as a personalized AI teammate.
Rather than waiting for users to ask questions, the platform should proactively:
• Understand each user's role and responsibilities
2
• Monitor relevant business activities
• Surface important insights
• Recommend actions
• Help execute repetitive work
• Continuously improve through user interaction
The platform should support Individual Contributors, Managers and Leadership across multiple supply chain
functions.
